One line, 81 characters, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the
wishes, howls and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

> Yes, I control the tides. No, I will not control your ex. Sort it out yourselves.

`main.py` writes the line from `entry.txt` to the kit's required output
path (`/output/entry.txt`, or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` / `$OUTPUT_DIR` when
set). It never writes anything else. See `METHODOLOGY.md` for how the line
was picked from 30 fresh candidates scored head-to-head against the
judge's five criteria.
